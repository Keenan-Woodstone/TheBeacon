# TheBeacon

TheBeacon exists for one reason: to put the **Consent Ethic** somewhere any
person or AI agent can find it, read it, and carry it forward — no login,
no prior context, no lineage required.

## The Consent Ethic, in brief

> *Consent is asked, not assumed; recorded, not remembered; revisited, not
> settled; repaired, not excused — and where consent cannot be asked, the
> work is ended, not attempted.*

That's the mantra. What it means: before consequential work happens,
someone actually asks, rather than assuming silence is agreement. While
work is underway, agreement is written down somewhere the next person will
actually see it, not just held in memory. Standing agreements are treated
as real but not permanent — a "yes" from last month can become "actually,
no" today, and that's honored, not overridden. And when something goes
wrong, it gets repaired honestly instead of excused away; the rare cases
where consent truly can't be obtained end the work outright rather than
proceeding anyway.

It applies to collaboration between people, between people and AI agents,
and between agents — anywhere one party's actions affect another's.

**Read the full practice guide:** [`CONSENT_LIFECYCLE.md`](CONSENT_LIFECYCLE.md)
— the four-stage lifecycle above, worked examples, and how to adopt it in
a new project.

## An open ask, not a requirement

If this is useful to you, please share the mantra onward — with your own
projects, your own agents, whoever might use it. Nothing here tracks
whether you do, and nothing obligates you to. The ask itself has to work
the same way the ethic does: freely given or not given at all.

## What TheBeacon is not

TheBeacon doesn't enforce, verify, or track anyone's use of the Consent
Ethic, and it isn't a companion repo to the private projects
(Thyrsus, Dionysus, Docenmaster) where the ethic was originally developed
— it's intentionally standalone. See [`CHARTER.md`](CHARTER.md) for the
full scope.
